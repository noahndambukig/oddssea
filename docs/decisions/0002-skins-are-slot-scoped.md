# 0002 — Skins are scoped to a slot, not to a garment

**Decision:** A skin applies to *any* gear in its slot. "Void Weave headgear" works on all eleven headgear items.

**Why:** Garment-scoped skins multiply authoring cost by the garment count and mean every new garment ships with an empty skin library. Slot-scoping means a new garment inherits the entire existing skin catalogue for free on the day it ships.

**Exception:** Legendary and Mythic skins may be garment-specific, where exclusivity is the point and the cost is justified.

**Consequence:** Sets are defined as skin families spanning the six slots, which is why two players wearing the same set still look different — their underlying gear differs. This preserves personal expression under set-chasing.
