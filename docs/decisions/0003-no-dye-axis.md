# 0003 — No dye axis

**Decision:** Dyes are removed entirely. Players cannot recolour gear. Appearance is determined solely by the skin they equip.

**Why:** Player-controlled recolouring undermines the thing that makes the system work — that a skin's appearance is a reliable signal of what it cost to obtain. If any player can approximate a Legendary's palette with a cheap dye, the rarity ladder stops being readable at a glance, which is pillar 2. It also added a third currency sink to balance and a third axis to the dex for relatively little player-facing depth.

**What was removed:** Dye vials, dye application costs, the dye tier, dye-recipe first-discovery bounties, and dyes from the Phase 3 build order.

**What replaces the sink it provided:** Fusion (see `02-economy/currency-model.md`) and, later, the wear/condition axis. Both consume items as well as coins, so they are stronger sinks than dyes were.

**Revisit if:** Post-launch data shows players with large balances and nothing to spend on, *and* the wear axis hasn't shipped. Even then, prefer a constrained "recolour within this skin's own ramp" over free recolouring.
