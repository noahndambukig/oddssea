# 0013 — Gear is organised in families, and the launch families are chosen

**Decision:** Gear mirrors the skin structure: a **gear family is one theme
with exactly one garment in each of the six slots**, and each family has a
tier. The launch roster is 5 Common + 3 Rare + 2 Epic + 1 Legendary
families — 11 families × 6 slots = the same 66 garments the roster always
targeted, chosen as 11 themes instead of 66 independent items.

The chosen families: **Basics, Athletics, Skater, Picnic, Cozy** (Common) ·
**Streetwear, Aviator, Winter** (Rare) · **Deep Sea, Carnival** (Epic) ·
**Cosmic** (Legendary). Full derived roster in
`03-cosmetics/content/gear-roster.md`.

**Families are thematic, not economic.** Gear stays the freely-mixable
shape axis (`decisions/0001`, `0002`): no gear-set bonuses, no
family-completion rewards. Sets remain skin families only. A gear family
is coherence, shop presentation and marketing — nothing more.

**Why:** Choosing 66 independent items produced decision fatigue and a
roster with no narrative. Family-level selection keeps the tier arithmetic
identical, gives every slot a coherent look at each tier, makes shop
rotation legible ("Aviator week"), and — because one item per slot per
family — guarantees any family can be worn as a full outfit, which the
slot-by-slot candidate lists could not promise. Candidates considered and
not picked (Campus, Coastal, Commuter, Retro Leisure, Festival, Trail,
Rainy Day, Ballpark, Garden, Night Shift · Expedition, Angler, Varsity,
Courier, Ranch, Busker · Racing, Woodland, Workshop, Ceremony · Leviathan,
Clockwork) stay in the candidate bank as backlog for catalogue expansion
(`decisions/0011`).

**Consequence:** `gear-candidates.md` becomes the backlog; the roster of
record is `gear-roster.md`. The Legendary endgame look is now *designed*
as one outfit (full Cosmic) rather than six unrelated maxima — the
"Legendaries fight each other" risk from the candidate doc is resolved by
construction. One physical conflict needs a hide-flag: the hover skirt
conceals the anti-grav boots. Notably absent by choice: the racing jacket
(the pool's best flat skin canvas) — the bomber and tailcoat carry that
job now. Roadmap question 1 is resolved; art is unblocked pending the
skin-family pick (question 2).
