# 0010 — High crate volume, 1% Legendaries, and a premium crate tier

**Decision:** The first-Legendary tension (decision 0009's rarity retune vs
a fast honeymoon) is resolved by **volume, not a newbie guarantee**:

- A dedicated player opens **~50 basic crates per week** (casual ~20 at the
  established 2.5:1 income ratio). Crate opening is a high-frequency
  routine, not an event.
- Basic crates carry a **~1% Legendary rate**. At 50/week that puts the
  expected first Legendary at ~2 weeks for a dedicated player (~5 weeks
  casual), while the **200-crate pity** genuinely fires — roughly 13% of
  Legendary chases reach it.
- **Crates come in two quality tiers: basic and premium.** Premium costs
  several times more and carries boosted Epic and Legendary odds — the
  choice is volume (breadth, salvage fodder) versus concentration (chase
  quality). Premium gets its own, shorter pity. Exact rates and prices
  come from the simulations.
- The quality tier applies to Gear and Skin crates; the Set Crate stays
  single-tier — its value is targeting, not odds.

**Why:** Noah's insight: instead of a one-time newbie guarantee patching a
rare-drop economy, raise crate throughput until a rare per-crate rate
still produces a fast first Legendary. One mechanism fewer, and the
honeymoon comes from the same system as the endgame. The newbie guarantee
was considered and dropped as unnecessary. High volume also makes the
collection game's texture right for a permanent pool: lots of Commons
flowing through salvage into targeted purchases.

**Consequence:** Crate prices in Pearls must be low relative to Pearl
income (the sims tune to ~50/~20 opens per week); at this volume a
bulk-salvage UI becomes necessary (manual per-item salvage at ~50 opens a
week is tedium, not drama — the high-tier confirmations from 0009
stay). Coupon-collector math still protects the catalogue: ~1 Legendary
per ~2 weeks dedicated is ~26/year, but collecting all distinct
Legendaries takes far longer through duplicates. All four simulation
anchors are now set: crate pace 50/20, comeback floor ~35 minimum bets,
first Legendary ~2wk/~5wk via 1% + volume, classic edge split (races
~10%, instant 2–4%, roulette ~2.7%, blackjack ~1.5%).
