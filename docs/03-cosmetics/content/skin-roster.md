# Skin Roster

11 families × 6 slot definitions = 66 skins. A family is one treatment
described once and cut to each garment's mask; tier treatments and motion
budgets are set by `../animation-ladder.md` and `../rarity-tiers.md`.

## Common — flat fills, no motion

| Family | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Chalk** | Matte off-white, faint paper grain. The light neutral |
| **Ink** | Flat near-black with one sharp accent line. The dark neutral |
| **Cherrywood** | Deep red-brown with cream |
| **Buttercream** | Soft pale yellow and white |
| **Regatta** | Crisp navy and white |

## Rare — static materials

| Family | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Chrome Dip** | Mirrored metal with a hard specular band |
| **Tiger Maple** | Polished figured wood grain |
| **Denim Raw** | Woven indigo with contrast stitching |

Three genuinely distinct materials — metal, wood, fabric.

## Epic — one subtle looping property each

| Family | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Tidepool** | Slow blue-green gradient drift under a wet sheen |
| **Bloomcycle** | Printed flowers that slowly open and close |

Both organic by choice — the launch look is warm and natural; a
technical-motion Epic (Circuit Trace) stays in the backlog for expansion.

## Legendary — the cosmic

**Void Weave** — pure black garment with stars visible *through* it, as
though the clothing is a hole cut in reality. The starfield holds still
while the avatar moves. Particles: slow motes drifting from the "hole."
Equip flourish: the garment darkens to nothing, then stars fade in.

## Build notes

- **Test full Void Weave early.** The family exists in all six slots and
  will be worn as a complete outfit; six overlapping star-holes is a very
  different image from one. This is the launch catalogue's most important
  asset — over-invest.
- **Bloomcycle is the priciest non-Legendary** — printed flowers that
  animate are real art. Budget accordingly.
- **The palette is deliberately tasteful.** No high-vis loud Common made
  the cut (Traffic stays in the backlog); Regatta is the boldest base.
  If early players read the Commons as flat, the loud one is the first
  expansion candidate.

Struck candidates remain in `skin-candidates.md` as the expansion backlog
(`decisions/0011`).
