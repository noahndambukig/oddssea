# Sets

> **The structural ambiguity below is resolved.** v1 has no seasons
> (`decisions/0008`); launch sets are three permanent-pool families given
> a Legendary keystone piece — designated in `skin-roster.md`. The
> mixed-tier seasonal concepts below are the backlog for when seasons

> keep the cosmic out of any set was adopted.

## First, a structural ambiguity to resolve

Two things in this design are both called "a skin family spanning six slots," and they are not the same thing:

**The permanent pool** — the 11 launch skin families, now chosen and listed in `skin-roster.md`. Each family has **one uniform tier** (a Common family is Common in all six slots). These drop from Skin Crates and are always available.

**Seasonal sets** — themed families where the six slot-pieces have **mixed tiers**, with one Legendary keystone as the wall. These come from Set Crates, run for one season, then rotate to the vault.

Both are useful and they do different jobs. The permanent pool gives new players an immediate, affordable wardrobe; seasonal sets give committed players a time-bounded chase with a real completion moment.

**Recommendation:** keep the cosmic Legendary in the **permanent pool** rather than making it Season 1's set. It should be the aspirational anchor that is always visible and always chaseable — the thing a new player sees on day one and wants by day ninety. A Legendary that expires can never play that role.

**Decision needed from you.** If you'd rather have exactly one construct, the simpler option is to drop the permanent pool's tier uniformity and make every family a mixed-tier set. It's cleaner conceptually but leaves new players with nothing cheap to wear.

## Candidate seasonal sets

Each is six pieces: five Common-to-Epic plus one Legendary keystone.

| Set | Theme | Legendary keystone | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emberforge** | Volcanic, blacksmith | Held item: forge lantern | Flowing lava |
| **Deep Current** | Abyssal, bioluminescent | Backpack: pressurised tank | Running water + glow |
| **Neon Circuit** | Synthwave, retro-tech | Headgear: visor | Scrolling circuitry |
| **Static Ghost** | Glitch, corrupted signal | Shirt: torn jacket | Datamosh flicker |
| **Bloomdrift** | Overgrown, floral | Shoes: root-wrapped boots | Drifting petals |
| **Frostwake** | Winter, aurora | Shirt: frost-rimed coat | Aurora gradient pan |
| **Paper Lantern** | Festival, warm night | Held item: paper lantern | Soft flame flicker |
| **Sunfall** | Golden hour, desert tech | Pants: solar-panelled cargos | Heat shimmer |

Eight candidates is roughly two years of seasons at one per quarter. You need Season 1 locked and Season 2 sketched; the rest is backlog.

**Emberforge is the strongest opener** — lava is the most legible animated material at small sizes, it contrasts hardest against the cosmic Legendary in the permanent pool, and "flowing lava" is the effect most likely to make someone screenshot their avatar in week one.
