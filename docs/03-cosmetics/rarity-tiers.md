# Rarity Tiers

Five tiers. **Tier means rarity of acquisition, never power.** The moment Legendary implies "better at the game," pay-to-win pressure enters a cosmetic system and never leaves.

| Tier | Colour | Share of catalogue | Per-slot count at launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | Grey | ~45% | 5 |
| **Rare** | Blue | ~27% | 3 |
| **Epic** | Purple | ~18% | 2 |
| **Legendary** | Gold | ~9% | 1 |
| **Mythic** | Prismatic | ~1% | 0 — not in the crate catalogue |

Applies identically to both axes: 5 / 3 / 2 / 1 gear per slot, and 5 / 3 / 2 / 1 skin families overall.

## The rule that makes tiers readable

**Legendary and Mythic gear must change the silhouette, not just the colour.**

A rare recolour is invisible at 64px in a lobby. A rare *shape* — oversized headgear, a trailing hem, absurd platform boots — reads instantly at any size. This is the difference between a rarity system players perceive and one they have to click to verify.

For skins, the equivalent rule is the animation ladder — see `animation-ladder.md`.

## Mythic: one-of-one

Mythic items are single instances. Exactly one exists across the entire app, ever.

- **Never sold in crates.** Sources: seasonal tournament placement, first-discovery bounties, anniversary awards, one-of-one auctions, and the first player to complete each season's set.
- **Provenance is displayed.** The item permanently shows who has owned it, in order. When a Mythic changes hands it should feel like news.
- Budget 6–12 Mythics per year. Scarcity is the entire product here.
