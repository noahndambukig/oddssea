# Design Pillars

In priority order. When two decisions conflict, the higher pillar wins.

## 1. Give currency somewhere to go

The catalogue must always contain something the player wants and cannot yet afford. A player who runs out of things to want stops caring about the currency, and shortly afterwards stops caring about earning it.

## 2. Make status readable at a glance

A rare item nobody notices is not a flex. Rarity must be legible from a thumbnail, at a distance, in a feed. This pillar is why Legendaries break silhouette and why motion starts at Epic.

## 3. Reward depth, not just spend

Completion — a finished set, a filled dex page — should be worth more than owning one flashy item. Collecting should beat cherry-picking.

## 4. Stay cheap to produce

Cosmetic systems die when each new item costs an artist a day. Content velocity is a design constraint, not an afterthought. This pillar is why skins are slot-scoped and why the animation ladder is one shader with a parameter table.

## 5. Never inflate

Any mechanic that returns currency must consume more than it produces. This pillar is why set completion pays a one-time bonus rather than a perpetual stipend, and why the house edge must absorb the task faucet.
