# Gear Candidates

**Target roster: 11 items per slot** — 5 Common, 3 Rare, 2 Epic, 1 Legendary. Six slots, so 66 garments at launch.

Each list below is deliberately over-supplied so there is a real choice to make. Pick 11 per slot; strike the rest. Anything struck stays in this file as a backlog for later seasons rather than being deleted.

**★ = my recommendation** for that slot's roster.

## How the tiers should feel in gear

| Tier | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Common** | Ordinary clothing. The stuff a player wears on day one and still wears at day 200 with a good skin on it. Must be a clean, simple shape. |
| **Rare** | Recognisably *chosen*. A bit of character, a bit more geometry, but still grounded. |
| **Epic** | Distinctive and slightly strange. Reads as a statement from across a lobby. |
| **Legendary** | **Breaks the silhouette.** If it doesn't change the avatar's outline at 64px, it isn't a Legendary — it's an Epic with a gold border, and players will notice the difference immediately. |

## 1. Headgear

The highest-attention slot. Spend your best ideas here.

**Common** — pick 5
★ Forward cap · ★ Beanie · ★ Bucket hat · ★ Bandana · ★ Visor · Newsboy cap · Earmuffs · Headband · Backwards snapback

**Rare** — pick 3
★ Over-ear headphones · ★ Aviator goggles · ★ Hood (up) · Trapper hat · Welding mask (flipped up) · Bike helmet · Beret

**Epic** — pick 2
★ Antique brass diving helmet · ★ Fox-ear hood · Miner's lantern helm (casts a light cone) · Bubble helmet · Paper festival mask · Plague-doctor beak (probably too grim for your tone)

**Legendary** — pick 1
★ **Orbiting halo ring** — a ring that hovers and slowly rotates above the head. Reads instantly at any size, is trivially cheap (one sprite plus a rotation), and is the single most recognisable "this player has something" signal you can build.
· **Floating constellation** — three or four small stars orbiting the head on different radii. Pairs beautifully with the cosmic Legendary skin.
· **Comet crest** — a long streaming plume trailing behind the head, animated with a two-frame sway.
· **Satellite dish helm** — absurd, funny, huge silhouette change.
· **Antler crown** — organic, elegant, big outline.

**Epic** — pick 2

**Legendary** — pick 1

## Consistency check before you lock this in

Three things to look at once you've picked:

**Do your six Legendaries fight each other?** They will frequently be worn together by your most invested players. Halo ring + winged coat + hover skirt + anti-grav boots + wing rig + star net is a *lot* happening at once. Either accept that as the intended endgame look, or deliberately pick a couple of quieter Legendaries so the full-Legendary loadout still reads.

**Watch for physical contradictions.** Hover skirt hides the feet, so anti-grav boots are invisible underneath it. Balloon cluster and anti-grav boots both lift the avatar. Wing rig and jetpack both occupy the rear layer. Note conflicts now and resolve them with hide-flags rather than discovering them in QA.

**Check the Commons are boring enough.** This sounds backwards, but Commons exist to be the neutral base that makes a good skin shine. If your Commons are too characterful they fight the skin, and players end up with 66 items that all look busy.
